System Longevity & Performance Care

Radon System Maintenance & Diagnostics

Keep your active soil depressurization system operating at peak negative pressure with factory-calibrated fan replacements, pipe re-sealing, and biennial verification testing.

U-Tube Manometer Check

The red oil in your U-tube manometer should never be level. A difference in oil heights confirms active suction is drawing vacuum underneath your slab.

Centrifugal Fan Lifespan

Radon fans run continuously 24/7/365. Average lifespan is 5 to 10 years. If your fan begins humming loudly, vibrating, or shuts off, replacement is vital.

Slab Seal Integrity

Polyurethane elastomeric caulking seals around sump basins and floor joints can settle over time. We re-caulk cracks to maintain sub-slab suction.

Book a Diagnostic System Checkup

Experiencing zero liquid differential on your U-tube manometer or need your mandatory 2-year post-mitigation re-test? Our certified technicians can evaluate your system in under an hour.
