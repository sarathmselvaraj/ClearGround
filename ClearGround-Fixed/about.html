Our Company & Philosophy

A Scientific Approach to Indoor Air Quality

ClearGround was established with a clear mandate: to approach radon measurement and mitigation not as generic handyman work, but as specialized building physics and pressure engineering.

Radon gas cannot be detected by scent, taste, or sight. Because soil gas migration varies with soil density, foundation porosity, and weather patterns, effective solutions require standardized continuous measurement protocols and carefully calculated sub-slab depressurization fields.

Our Mission

To provide transparent, defensible radon data and engineered fresh-air solutions that keep indoor air as clean as reasonably achievable.

Our Standard

Every testing procedure follows established public health and environmental protocols using calibrated continuous digital instruments.
