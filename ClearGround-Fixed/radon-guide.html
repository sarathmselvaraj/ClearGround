EPA Radon Reference Guide

The Complete Homeowner's Radon Guide

A definitive building-science guide to understanding radioactive soil gas, health effects, testing accuracy, and state-of-the-art sub-slab mitigation systems.

1. Geology & Physics

Radon-222 is an invisible, odorless, chemically inert radioactive gas generated by the radioactive decay of uranium naturally found in rock, soil, and gravel formations.

2. Foundation Pressure

Heated indoor air rises, creating a low-pressure vacuum effect (stack effect) that suctions soil gases into living areas through foundation pores, cracks, and joints.

3. EPA Health Threshold

Radon exposure is the #1 leading cause of lung cancer among non-smokers in the United States, responsible for approximately 21,000 preventable deaths every year.

EPA Action Guidelines by Level

Radon Level EPA Risk Category Recommended Action
Below 2.0 pCi/L Low Ambient Risk No mitigation needed. Re-test every 2 to 3 years or after major renovations.
2.0 – 3.9 pCi/L Moderate Exposure Consider mitigation, especially for high occupancy basements and bedrooms.
4.0+ pCi/L Federal Action Level Immediate active mitigation strongly recommended by EPA and Surgeon General.
