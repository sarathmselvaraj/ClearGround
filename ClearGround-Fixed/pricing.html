Cost Transparency

Why Mitigation Pricing Varies by Foundation

Radon mitigation systems are customized to your home's structural footprint, foundation construction, and sub-slab aggregate. Here is what impacts the installation cost.

Type 01

Slab-on-Grade

$1,450 – $1,850

Single-level homes with living areas directly over concrete. Typically requires a single suction core through the slab with vertical exhaust run through a garage or exterior wall.

  • 1 suction pit excavation
  • Standard exterior fan housing
  • Fast 3 to 4 hour installation
Type 02

Poured Basement

$1,500 – $1,950

Full below-grade basements with poured concrete walls. Requires sealing of cold joints, floor drains, and sump basin covers alongside core pit depressurization.

  • Airtight polycarbonate sump cover
  • Slab perimeter polyurethane sealing
  • Attic or exterior roofline exhaust
Type 03

Crawlspace Encapsulation

$2,200 – $3,400

Exposed soil beneath floor joists requires sub-membrane depressurization (SMD), laying heavy 20-mil cross-laminated barrier sealed to foundation stems.

  • 20-mil virgin poly vapor membrane
  • Perimeter batten-strip mechanical seal
  • Perforated sub-membrane collector pipe
Type 04

Mixed / Split-Level

$2,400 – $3,800

Properties with multiple sub-structural elevations (partial basement plus addition on slab or crawlspace). Requires manifold piping connecting multiple suction zones.

  • Multiple sub-slab suction cavities
  • Pressure-balancing manifold valves
  • High-static commercial-grade fan
