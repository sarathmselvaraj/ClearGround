Diagnostic Engineering

Why Foundation Type Dictates Strategy

A one-size-fits-all approach does not work in radon remediation. Slabs with dense clay sub-soil require high-suction fans, while coarse gravel aggregate allows high-airflow, low-wattage fan models.

ClearGround conducts Pressure Field Extension (PFE) micro-vacuum testing before system completion. We drill small test points across opposite ends of the foundation to confirm the suction envelope completely blankets the entire footprint.
