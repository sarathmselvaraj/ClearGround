Buyer Guide

What Homebuyers Need to Know

A high radon reading does not mean you should walk away from a property you love. Radon is one of the most reliably solvable environmental issues in real estate. With an active soil depressurization system, even very high levels can be brought down effectively.

  • Always request a certified 48-hour continuous monitor test during the due diligence period.
  • Confirm closed-building conditions were observed by the occupants.
  • Use certified test reports to request seller concessions or escrow credit.

Understanding Your Inspection Report

Real estate transactions require clear, defensible data. ClearGround inspection reports are designed to be easily understood by buyers, sellers, lenders, and closing agents.

•

Overall Average Measurement

The primary test metric calculated from 48 consecutive hourly readings, expressed in picocuries per liter (pCi/L).

•

Hourly Concentration Chart

Constructive Guidance

What Happens When Test Results Are Elevated?

If a 48-hour real estate test returns at or above the EPA Action Level of 4.0 pCi/L, there is no need for panic. In residential real estate, elevated radon is a routine repair item handled through clear, standard procedures.

1. Review the Findings

Examine the 48-hour average alongside weather conditions and confirm testing protocol compliance.

2. Request a Fixed Bid

ClearGround provides rapid written mitigation estimates detailing system routing and guaranteed reduction.

3. Agree on Resolution

Buyer and seller agree on installation prior to closing or establish an escrow holdback fund at title.

4. Post-Mitigation Check

Following system installation, follow-up testing confirms that indoor radon levels have been successfully lowered.
