Active Sub-Slab Depressurization (ASD)

The most reliable, energy-efficient engineering solution to permanently stop radon soil gas from entering your living spaces.

Home / Services / Radon Mitigation Systems
Residential Testing Commercial Facilities Short-Term 48-Hr Long-Term Monitoring Mitigation Systems Post-Mitigation Check

Comprehensive Service Overview

Active Sub-Slab Depressurization works by creating a negative pressure field beneath the concrete slab that exceeds the natural vacuum pull of your home. By drawing soil gas directly from the sub-grade gravel bed and exhausting it safely above the roofline, radon never penetrates the living envelope.

Who This Service Is For

Homeowners with confirmed radon levels at or above 4.0 pCi/L, prospective homebuyers negotiating escrow repairs, and property owners seeking permanent long-term radon protection.

Execution & Technical Process

What This Service Includes

  • Professional on-site diagnostic assessment
  • Calibrated instrumentation and materials
  • Complete system routing and installation
  • Documentation and post-service report

Preparation & Considerations

  • Ensure clear access to basement floor area
  • Confirm proposed exterior or attic vent pathway
  • Plan for 3 to 5 hours of on-site technician time

Expected Results & Deliverables

A fully operational active mitigation system with visual pressure monitoring, sealed suction pit, quiet exterior centrifugal fan, and a post-mitigation verification test certificate confirming reduced indoor radon concentrations.

Installed Active Depressurization Assembly

Schedule-40 PVC, inline fan, and U-tube manometer pressure gauge.
