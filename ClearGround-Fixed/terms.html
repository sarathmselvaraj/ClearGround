Terms of Service

Contractual standards, performance warranties, closed-building requirements, and mitigation post-test commitments.

1. Professional Testing Protocols

All short-term radon tests are administered in strict compliance with ANSI/AARST protocol MAH 2019. Homeowners and real estate occupants must maintain closed-building conditions for a minimum of 12 hours prior to and during the entire 48-hour continuous measurement period.

2. Mitigation Guarantee & EPA Action Levels

ClearGround guarantees that our installed active soil depressurization (ASD) systems will reduce living space radon concentrations to below the EPA action level of 4.0 pCi/L (and aims for the WHO recommendation of under 2.0 pCi/L). If post-installation diagnostic testing exceeds 4.0 pCi/L, we install additional suction suction pits or fan upgrades at zero additional labor cost.

3. Fan Warranty & Electrical Components

Radon fans installed by ClearGround include a 5-year full manufacturer warranty and a 1-year ClearGround workmanship guarantee. Continuous operation is monitored via the visual U-tube manometer installed on the system riser pipe.

4. Real Estate Escrow Holdback Compliance

We supply binding mitigation quotes and certificate deliverables suitable for real estate closing contingencies and escrow documentation within 24 business hours of diagnostic completion.
